Credit policy for 36-month unsecured personal loans (written for this study; it is not the policy of any lender)

1. Debt-to-income ratio above 25% is a breach; above 20% is a concern.
2. Requested loan amount above 30% of annual income is a breach.
3. Two or more delinquencies in the last 2 years is a breach; one is a concern.
4. Three or more credit inquiries in the last 6 months is a breach.
5. Any derogatory public record is a breach.
6. Revolving utilisation above 80% is a breach; above 60% is a concern.
7. Credit history shorter than 3 years at the application date is a breach.
8. Employment length under 1 year, or not reported, is a concern.
9. Annual income that is not verified, combined with a loan amount above 15,000 USD, is a concern.
10. Loans for small business purposes are a concern.
11. Fewer than 3 open credit accounts is a concern.
12. An application with two or more breaches should normally be rated high risk; one with no breach and at most one concern should normally be rated low risk.
